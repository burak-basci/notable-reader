# Complete book fixture

**The Tale of Peter Rabbit**, Beatrix Potter (1866–1943), originally published
in 1902. Source: [Project Gutenberg ebook 14838](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/14838).

`peter-rabbit-complete-story.txt` contains the complete 950-word narrative from
its first sentence through THE END. It omits only illustration placeholders
and non-story publisher/licence material. It is not a chapter, excerpt, or
truncated preview. The trailing newline is retained in the exported text.

`peter-rabbit-gutenberg-original.txt` preserves the full downloaded source,
including its original Project Gutenberg licence/trademark notices. This
public-domain fixture is separate from the MIT adapter and LGPL encoder.
`source-provenance.json` records exact URLs, author, hashes, byte counts,
character counts and the extraction rule. No private user book is included.
